Zor Data

Internal data collection for the Zor platform

Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 24, 2026

Zor Data is an internal mobile application for authorized staff who collect and maintain information for the Zor platform. Accounts are issued by your office. This policy explains how information is handled when you sign in, manage catalogue data, and browse or create administrative areas. In this policy, “we” refers to the organization operating your Zor service and issuing your account.

1. Information processed by the app

  • Account information: your sign-in email and account information returned by the service, which may include your user ID, name, display name, phone number and verification status, preferred language, account type, account status, and profile-completion status.
  • Authentication information: your password when signing in, access and refresh tokens for your session, and your email, recovery challenge identifier, verification code, and new password when you use password recovery.
  • Catalogue submissions: category, tag, and sector names and descriptions, record identifiers, and changes or review submissions you make through the app.
  • Geographic records: administrative-area names, administrative levels, parent areas, optional codes, and descriptions you submit. These describe geographic areas rather than automatically recording your device location.
  • Search and connection information: search terms, filters, and pagination details sent to the service. Network requests also expose your IP address and request details to the receiving infrastructure; operational or security logs may retain these details.

Only include information needed for your assigned collection work. Avoid placing unrelated personal or sensitive information in record names, descriptions, or search terms.

2. How information is used

Information is used to authenticate authorized staff, maintain sessions, recover account access, display account details, retrieve and search reference data, and create, rename, submit for review, or delete catalogue records where permitted. Geographic submissions are used to maintain the platform’s administrative-area hierarchy. Submitted records are sent to the Zor backend so they can be stored, reviewed, and used in the platform’s data workflows.

Account and request information also supports service administration, troubleshooting, security, and responses to support requests.

3. Location and device permissions

The current app declares location permissions and includes a service that can request your current location while the app is in use. The current catalogue and geography screens do not call that service or attach GPS coordinates to their submissions. Creating an administrative-area record does not itself collect your device’s location.

If a location-enabled workflow is made available, the location service requests operating-system permission before obtaining a current position. Location access can reveal your approximate or precise position depending on your device settings. The current app does not implement continuous or background location tracking. You can deny or revoke location permission in device settings.

The current collection workflows do not request camera, microphone, contacts, or photo-library access and do not upload captured photos or recordings.

4. Local storage and online processing

The app uses the device’s secure-storage facility to retain access and refresh tokens and account identifiers such as your email and user ID. It does not save your sign-in password through this storage facility. Online features send requests to the configured Zor service; catalogue and geographic submissions are stored on the server rather than in an offline submission database in the current app.

Signing out clears the locally stored session information and attempts to end the server session. Signing out or uninstalling the app does not delete your server account or submitted records. Device backup, restore, and secure-storage behavior depend on your operating system.

5. Access and disclosure

Information sent through the app is processed by the Zor backend and the infrastructure used to operate it. Authorized administrators, reviewers, and other personnel may access information as needed for their assigned work, service support, or security. Catalogue and geographic records are platform data and may be available to other Zor users through the platform’s access and publication workflows; they should not be treated as private notes.

Information may also be disclosed where required by applicable law or necessary to investigate abuse or protect the service and its users. The current mobile app does not integrate advertising, advertising identifiers, or third-party analytics SDKs.

6. Retention and deletion

Account information and submitted records are retained by the organization operating your Zor service for its collection, review, administration, and security needs and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. Retention may differ for account data, platform records, operational logs, and backups. This policy does not specify a fixed retention period.

Where your permissions allow it, the app provides deletion of catalogue records. This does not delete your account or guarantee immediate removal from logs or backups. The current app does not provide self-service account deletion or editing of account profile details.

To request access to, correction of, or deletion of account information or submitted personal information, contact the office administrator who issued your account. Identify the account and records concerned. Your organization may need to verify your identity and determine whether records must be retained before acting on a request.

7. Security and processing location

The app uses authenticated API requests and device secure storage for session credentials. Protect your device and account credentials and sign out when using a shared device. No method of storage or transmission can guarantee absolute security.

Information is processed on your device and on the infrastructure configured for your organization’s Zor service. Contact your administrator for details of the service operator, hosting location, service providers, and any processing outside your country.

8. Your choices and responsibilities

You can manage location permissions in device settings, sign out of the app, and ask your administrator to correct account information or review your access. Submit only information you are authorized to collect and follow your organization’s procedures when collecting information about other people or businesses.

9. Children’s privacy

Zor Data is intended for authorized staff using office-issued accounts and is not directed at children. Report any unnecessary personal information about a child to your administrator so it can be reviewed for removal.

10. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated as Zor Data’s collection workflows and data practices change. The latest version will show its update date at the top of this page.

11. Contact

For privacy questions, account requests, or concerns about submitted information, contact the administrator or internal support channel of the office that issued your Zor Data account. Ask for the person responsible for privacy for your Zor service. Identify your request as a “Zor Data privacy request” and include your account email and relevant record identifiers. Do not send passwords, verification codes, or session tokens.
